# Waqfa-AI
Waqfa AI is an AI-powered gap-year planning assistant designed to help students plan their gap year so it aligns with their career goals and future aspirations. 
# Project Name: Waqfa AI
## Problem
 Every year, many Pakistani students take a gap year, either by choice or because they couldn't get into their dream university. However, without a clear plan and proper guidance, many students waste this valuable time and their potential. 
## Solution
By asking about a student's academic background, interests, career goals, and available resources, Waqfa AI generates a detailed month-to-month roadmap. Rather than offering generic advice, Waqfa AI understands each student's situation and creates a structured, accessible, and actionable guide.
## Features
1. Student Blueprint: Collects information about a student's academic background, interests, future goals, and available resources to create a personalized roadmap.
2. AI Roadmap Generator: Generates a detailed month-to-month roadmap based on the student's circumstances, with structured goals and actionable steps.
3. Application Tracker: Provides a space to organize and track university applications, entry test dates, and requirements in one place.
4. Skills Builder: Helps students identify relevant skills to develop and explore learning opportunities aligned with future goals.
5. AI Mentor: A chatbot that helps students ask questions, understand and adapt their roadmap to their needs, and consider adjustments when needed. 
## Tech Stack
1. Development Environment: Google AI Studio
2. AI Model: Google Gemini
3. Deployment: Google AI Studio
## AI / API Usage
Waqfa AI uses Google's Gemini AI to generate personalized gap-year roadmaps based on entered data. 
## Setup Instructions
1. Clone or download this repository
2. Install project dependencies
3. Configure the required environment variables and AI credentials.
4. Run the project's development command
5. Open the local URL provided by the development server.
